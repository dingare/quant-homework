### Answer and Feedback

- Result: **P/Y**. This was an **intentional spaced-repetition revisit** of the prior day's topological-sort miss.
- Major improvement: independently retrieved adjacency lists, indegrees, a zero-indegree queue, and BFS / Kahn topological processing.
- Missing step: dynamic programming over the DAG. For each edge `u -> v`, propagate `finish[v] = max(finish[v], finish[u] + duration[v])`.
